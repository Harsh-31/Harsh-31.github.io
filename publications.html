Research Reports

Course and independent research projects with written reports.

  1. Report
    GroundedVLA: 3D Spatial Grounding for Vision-Language-Action Models
    Harsh Sharma
    Graduate research project, University of Southern California
    Post-trained 9 LoRA-adapted π0.5 VLA policies on 6 LIBERO-90 tasks with a ~5M-parameter adapter for 2D/3D spatial inputs, using a multimodal training set of 300 demonstrations (~37K frames) with validated camera projections. Behavior evaluations over ~5,000 perturbed episodes showed baseline success falling from 99% to 22.5%, and 720 counterfactual tests traced failures to policies ignoring the spatial inputs.
  2. Report
    Robot Imitation Learning: A Data Efficiency Study with Action Chunking Transformers
    Harsh Sharma
    Graduate research project, University of Southern California
    Trained a PPO expert to 98% success on simulated Franka Panda cube lifting and collected 110 demonstrations. Nine ACT policies trained on 25, 50, and 100 demos raised mean success from 14% to 69% across 100 unseen starts. Uncorrected missed grasps remained a key failure mode, showing that similarity to training demonstrations alone did not ensure success.
  3. Report
    Curvature-Guided Rank Selection for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning
    Harsh Sharma
    Graduate research project, University of Southern California
    A 14-rank LoRA sweep on ViT-Base/16 for CIFAR-100 with diagonal Fisher curvature analysis found that Fisher trace correlates strongly with test accuracy. An online curvature-guided rank adaptation method achieved competitive accuracy with fewer average parameters than fixed-rank baselines.
