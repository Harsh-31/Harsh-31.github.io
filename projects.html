GroundedVLA: 3D Spatial Grounding for VLAs

Post-trained 9 LoRA-adapted π0.5 policies on 6 LIBERO-90 tasks with a ~5M-param adapter for 2D/3D spatial inputs. Across ~5,000 perturbed episodes, baseline success fell from 99% to 22.5%; 720 counterfactual tests traced failures to ignored spatial inputs.
